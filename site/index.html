Unofficial desktop player for TIDAL

A TIDAL player you can see through

Idolize lets your wallpaper shine through its window, and gives every song its own light show. Free and open source, for Windows.

The player

Every song gets a light show

Open the player and the album cover becomes an LED wall that follows the song. Idolize reads the song ahead of time, so the big moments land on the beat.

1:09 – 1:27
  1. 1:13Build-upThe wall draws in and it starts to rain.
  2. 1:19DropThe album bursts apart.
  3. 1:20PeakA kaleidoscope made from the cover, once per song.
What's in it
  • See-through windowClear, Acrylic or Mica, with a tint you set.
  • Transparent playerYour desktop behind the player, sharp or blurred.
  • A light show for every songThe album cover as an LED wall, in sync with the music.
  • Lyrics and commentsSynced lyrics and the song's top YouTube comments.
  • Hi-res losslessUp to 24-bit FLAC with your TIDAL subscription.
  • Free and open sourceMIT license, built in the open on GitHub.
